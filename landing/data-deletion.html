Data Deletion Request

Est8Go Services Limited  ·  We respond within 30 days

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data from Est8Go's systems at any time. This includes your conversation history, contact information, and any other data we hold about you.

What gets deleted

  • Your WhatsApp conversation history with our bot (Kora)
  • Your name, phone number, and contact details
  • Your property search history and preferences
  • Any lead records associated with your number

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to est8go@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include your WhatsApp phone number (the number you used to message our bot) so we can identify your records
  3. We will confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 days
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently deleted
