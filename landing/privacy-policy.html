Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026  ·  Est8Go Services Limited
Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Est8Go collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our platform and services.

1. Who We Are

Est8Go Services Limited ("Est8Go", "we", "our") operates a multi-tenant real estate technology platform that connects property buyers with verified real estate agencies and independent realtors in Nigeria. Our registered address is Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, email address when you interact with our platform or bot
  • Property enquiry data — property preferences, location interests, budget range shared during conversations
  • WhatsApp messages — conversations between you and our AI assistant (Kora) via WhatsApp Business API
  • Device and usage data — browser type, IP address, pages visited, timestamps
  • Business account data — company name, realtor profiles, listing information for registered agencies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect you with verified property listings and real estate agents
  • Power our AI assistant (Kora) to answer property enquiries
  • Send lead alerts to realtors when you express interest in a property
  • Verify property listings and generate trust scores
  • Improve our platform, services, and AI accuracy
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. WhatsApp and Meta Data

Est8Go uses the WhatsApp Business API, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you message us on WhatsApp, your messages are processed by Meta in accordance with their privacy policy. We only use your WhatsApp data to respond to your property enquiries and connect you with realtors.

We do not use your WhatsApp data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With registered agencies — your name and phone number are shared with the realtor assigned to a listing when you express interest
  • Service providers — we use Supabase (database), OpenAI (AI), Resend (email), and Paystack (payments) as infrastructure providers
  • Legal requirements — when required by Nigerian law or court order

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain your conversation data for 90 days from your last interaction. Business account data is retained for the duration of the account and 12 months after account closure. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at est8go@gmail.com

8. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, visit est8go.com/data-deletion or email est8go@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, role-based access controls, and regular security audits to protect your information.

10. Cookies

Our web platform uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email. Continued use of our platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
