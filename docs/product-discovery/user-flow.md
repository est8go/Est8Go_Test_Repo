# Kora Pilot User Flow

Main goal: a buyer who sends a WhatsApp enquiry receives a prompt response, supplies the basic details needed to qualify the enquiry, and is handed to a responsible agency team member with a follow-up task that cannot be forgotten.

1. **Buyer sends an enquiry.** A buyer messages Bravies Homz’s WhatsApp business number, typically after seeing a property advert or social-media post.
2. **Kora captures the enquiry.** Kora receives the first message and creates a lead record with the buyer’s WhatsApp contact, message time, and conversation context. The lead starts as **New**.
3. **Buyer receives an immediate acknowledgement.** Kora sends the agency-approved first reply, including when the message arrives outside operating hours. The lead’s first-response time is recorded.
4. **Kora begins qualification.** Kora asks the buyer for the required information: name, preferred location, property type, budget if they choose to share it, and purchase timeline.
5. **Buyer answers the questions.** The buyer replies in WhatsApp. Kora adds each supplied answer to the lead record. If key information is still missing, Kora continues the qualification conversation.
6. **Kora marks the lead as qualified.** Once the required information is captured, Kora changes the lead status from **Awaiting qualification** to **Qualified**.
7. **Kora hands the lead to the agency team.** Kora assigns or routes the qualified lead to the responsible salesperson or customer-service representative and notifies that person that action is needed.
8. **Assigned team member reviews the lead.** The team member opens the lead list, reads the buyer’s messages and qualification details, and sees the lead owner, status, response time, and next action.
9. **Team member contacts the buyer.** The assigned person continues the conversation with the buyer, answers specific property questions, and agrees the next human-led action, such as an inspection discussion. Kora does not negotiate or close the deal.
10. **Kora creates a follow-up task.** After handoff, Kora records a follow-up task with the assigned owner and due time. The lead status becomes **Handed off**.
11. **Team member completes or updates the follow-up.** The team member contacts the buyer by the due time and updates the lead’s next action or marks it **Closed** when no further action is needed.
12. **Kora reminds when action is overdue.** If the owner has not completed the follow-up by its due time, Kora notifies them. The sales manager or founder can see the overdue item in the lead list.

## Completion state

The main flow is complete when a qualified buyer has been handed to a named agency team member and that owner has either completed the required follow-up or deliberately closed the lead. The pilot measures the recorded first-response time, qualification, assignment, and overdue follow-ups; it does not claim that Kora caused a sale.
