Pilot user flow

From a buyer’s first WhatsApp message to a completed human follow-up.

  1. Buyer sends an enquiry.A buyer messages Bravies Homz’s WhatsApp business number, usually after seeing a property advert or social-media post.
  2. Kora captures the enquiry.Kora creates a lead record with the buyer’s WhatsApp contact, message time, and conversation context. The lead starts as New.
  3. Buyer receives an immediate acknowledgement.Kora sends the agency-approved first reply, including outside operating hours, and records the first-response time.
  4. Kora begins qualification.Kora asks for the buyer’s name, preferred location, property type, budget if supplied, and purchase timeline.
  5. Buyer answers the questions.Kora adds supplied answers to the lead record and continues the qualification conversation if key information is missing.
  6. Kora marks the lead as qualified.Once the required information is captured, the lead moves from Awaiting qualification to Qualified.
  7. Kora hands the lead to the agency team.Kora assigns or routes the lead to the responsible salesperson or customer-service representative and notifies that person.
  8. Assigned team member reviews the lead.The team member opens the lead list and sees the buyer’s messages, qualification details, owner, status, response time, and next action.
  9. Team member contacts the buyer.The assigned person answers specific questions and agrees the next human-led action, such as an inspection discussion. Kora does not negotiate or close the deal.
  10. Kora creates a follow-up task.Kora records a task with an owner and due time. The lead status becomes Handed off.
  11. Team member completes or updates the follow-up.The owner contacts the buyer by the due time and updates the next action or marks the lead Closed.
  12. Kora reminds when action is overdue.If no follow-up is completed by its due time, Kora notifies the owner; the sales manager or founder can see it in the lead list.

Completion state

The flow completes when a qualified buyer is handed to a named agency team member and that owner completes the follow-up or deliberately closes the lead. The pilot records response, qualification, assignment, and overdue follow-ups; it does not claim that Kora caused a sale.
